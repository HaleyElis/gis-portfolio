Regional Mapping · Long Island, New York

Exploring the North Fork

A regional map bringing together the destinations, transportation connections, and physical landscape of Long Island’s North Fork.

ArcGIS Pro Cartographic design Data integration GEOG 455

Project overview

This project explores the North Fork as both a destination and a physical landscape. It brings together places to visit, transportation connections, terrain, and soils to provide a geographic portrait of the region.

The goal was to combine several kinds of information in a layout that remains inviting to explore and easy to read.

Mapping approach

I assembled and organized geographic layers in ArcGIS Pro, including destinations, roads, transportation facilities, elevation, and soils. Preparing these layers involved combining datasets and selecting features relevant to the map’s regional focus.

Elevation data provided terrain context, while soils added a perspective on the landscape’s relationship to viticulture. Transportation layers helped place destinations within the region’s road and travel network.

Cartographic design

The design balances destination information with geographic context. Distinct symbols identify different types of places, while the background layers establish the North Fork’s shape and setting.

Symbol hierarchy, labeling, and the arrangement of map elements help readers move between individual destinations and the broader landscape.

Course context

Created for GEOG 455 at UNC–Chapel Hill. This project combines GIS data preparation with regional cartography and map layout design.
