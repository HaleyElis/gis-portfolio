# Haley Meyrowitz | GIS Portfolio

A collection of my GIS, cartography, and spatial analysis projects.

**[View my GIS portfolio](https://haleyelis.github.io/gis-portfolio/)**

## About me

I am a geoscientist and graduate student at UNC–Chapel Hill,
pursuing an M.S. in Earth, Marine and Environmental Sciences
and a Graduate Certificate in GIS. My background includes
field geology, environmental consulting, research, and teaching.

## Projects

### Missed Connections
Exploring Amtrak service and geographic gaps in passenger rail
station access in North Carolina. Submitted to the 2026 UNC
GIS Day Map Competition.

[View project](https://haleyelis.github.io/gis-portfolio/missed-connections.html)

### Exploring the North Fork
Regional cartography bringing together destinations,
transportation, terrain, and soils on Long Island’s North Fork.

[View project](https://haleyelis.github.io/gis-portfolio/north-fork.html)

### Mapping Trail Proximity in Portland
Buffer analysis and an infographic examining land within
half a mile of existing trails in Portland’s Tri-Metro region.

[View project](https://haleyelis.github.io/gis-portfolio/portland-trails.html)

### Connecting Brooklyn and Queens — Coming soon
Investigating how the proposed Interborough Express could
expand walking access to rail stations.
