Missed Connections

Exploring the geography of passenger rail access in North Carolina: where Amtrak connects communities and where gaps remain.

ArcGIS Pro Spatial analysis Cartographic design 2026

The question

This project explores the relationship between Amtrak’s network and the locations where passengers can access it in North Carolina.

The map considers both the connections provided by existing service and the geographic gaps between communities and passenger stations.

Mapping approach

I used ArcGIS Pro to bring together passenger rail routes, station locations, and geographic context. The statewide map highlights North Carolina’s rail geography, while a national inset places these connections within the broader Amtrak network.

The layout uses a coordinated color palette and a clear visual hierarchy to distinguish the rail network, station access, and surrounding geography. Data sources and the map projection are documented in the map credits.

Reading the map

Passenger rail access depends on where stations are located, not simply on where tracks or routes run. Gaps in station access help reveal places where the presence of a rail corridor may not translate into a convenient opportunity to use passenger rail.

This map focuses on geographic access. It does not establish that every destination on the national network is reachable by a direct train from North Carolina, or measure service frequency, fares, or door-to-door travel time.
